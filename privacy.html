Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Passly (“we”, “us”, or “our”), operated by Bilal M., collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Passly mobile application (“App”).

1. Who we are

Passly is a digital wallet app that helps you create, store, customize, share, and manage passes (such as boarding passes, event tickets, coupons, and similar items).

Operator: Bilal M.

Contact: support.passly@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Account information: If you sign in with Apple, we receive an identifier from Apple and, if you choose to share them, your name and email.
  • Pass content: Pass details you create or import (titles, fields, barcodes, images, notes, and related metadata).
  • Sync and sharing data: If you enable iCloud and sharing, your passes and share-related data may be stored in your iCloud / CloudKit account and shared with people you invite.
  • Purchase information: If you subscribe to Passly Pro, Apple processes the payment. We (and our subscription partner RevenueCat) may receive non-sensitive subscription status information (for example, whether your Pro entitlement is active).
  • Device / diagnostics: Basic technical information needed to run the App (such as crash or logging data you or Apple may provide).

3. How we use information

  • To provide and improve Passly features (create, edit, store, sync, share, and display passes).
  • To authenticate you (Sign in with Apple) and sync across your devices.
  • To manage Passly Pro subscriptions and unlock Pro features.
  • To respond to support requests and protect the security of the App.

4. How information is stored and shared

  • On your device: Passes and preferences may be stored locally on your device.
  • iCloud / CloudKit: When signed in and sync/sharing is enabled, Apple stores data in your iCloud account under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.
  • Apple Wallet: If you add a pass to Apple Wallet, Apple handles that pass under Apple’s policies.
  • Subscriptions: Payments are processed by Apple. Subscription status may be verified via RevenueCat.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your pass content with third parties for advertising.

5. Sharing with other users

If you share a pass, the recipients you invite can view the shared pass content. You can stop sharing later; recipients may lose access when sharing is stopped.

6. Data retention

We retain information only as long as needed to provide the App. You can delete passes in the App. If you sign out or stop using iCloud sync, local and/or iCloud copies may remain until you delete them or manage them in Apple Settings / iCloud.

7. Children’s privacy

Passly is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices

  • You can use many features without signing in; sync and sharing require Sign in with Apple and iCloud.
  • You can delete passes, stop sharing, restore or cancel subscriptions in Apple Settings, and request help at support.passly@gmail.com.

9. International users

Your information may be processed on your device and through Apple/iCloud services in locations where Apple operates.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact
